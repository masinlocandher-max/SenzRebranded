# SENZ Cinematic Website Design Spec

## Objective

Build SENZ as a premium strategic-communications and technology brand, not a conventional digital agency template. The website should feel like one continuous physical environment controlled by scroll.

## Visual DNA

True white and deep navy alternate as the main environmental states. Cobalt optical glass, icy cyan edge light, brushed silver, mirror chrome, translucent acrylic, controlled bloom, and restrained volumetric light create the material system.

The result should feel architectural, tactile, and commercially premium. Avoid neon-grid sci-fi, floating dashboard clutter, excessive glass cards, generic AI imagery, and dense poster typography.

## Scene 01 — The Threshold

A luminous SENZ gallery with a monumental blue ribbon-S sculpture, reflective pale floor, transparent architectural fins, a distant city atmosphere, and generous negative space for the main message.

Visible copy:
- INFLUENCE THROUGH CLARITY
- BRANDS MOVE PEOPLE. WE MAKE IT HAPPEN.
- Start a Conversation
- Explore Our Work

## Scene 02 — The Clarity Engine

A blue communication ribbon passes through controlled glass nodes, visually representing strategy turning complexity into clarity.

Core services:
- Branding & Identity
- PR & Strategic Communications
- Social Media Management
- Content & Messaging
- Campaign Strategy
- Reputation & Visibility

## Scene 03 — The Digital Core

The environment shifts to deep navy. A glowing optical cube, abstract laptop, articulated chrome hand, and modular technology labels represent websites, automation, AI, applications, booking systems, and integrations.

## Scene 04 — White-Label Growth

The world opens back toward light. One core system expands into multiple customizable glass modules, representing products that partners can rebrand, resell, customize, and scale.

## Motion

Scroll controls the scene state. Transitions should feel like moving through one SENZ world, not jumping between unrelated slides. Pointer movement adds subtle parallax only. Reduced-motion users receive the same content without unnecessary motion.

## UX guardrails

- Keep all real copy and CTAs as HTML.
- Preserve strong negative space.
- Maintain readable contrast at every scroll position.
- Mobile must stay usable even when 3D details are simplified.
- No decorative content that competes with the offer.
