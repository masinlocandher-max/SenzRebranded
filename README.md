# SENZ Rebranded

Strategic Communications + Digital Solutions.

Live working build: https://senz-rebrand.higgsfield.app

## Current direction

SENZ is positioned around three commercial routes:

- Upgrade an existing business through SENZ Business Crew
- Build a digital or AI-enabled business through SENZ Launch
- Resell white-label digital products under the reseller's own brand

The current visual direction uses a deep SENZ navy environment, cobalt accents, chrome and smoked-glass material language, and a cinematic robotic-hand assembly sequence to represent SENZ putting business capabilities together.

The live build includes the Business Crew offer, digital product families, white-label reseller proposition, SENZ Launch, and Francine Marie Bautista as Strategic Consultant.
